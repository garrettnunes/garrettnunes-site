        02 — Approach
        
How I build

        

          The same principles show up in everything — whether it’s an AI
          agent or a parts catalog.
        

      


      

        

          01
          
Automate the boring parts first

          
The highest-leverage software replaces the work nobody wants to do twice. Find the repetition, encode it, move on.

        

        

          02
          
Systems over heroics

          
A process that runs without me is worth more than a great day of manual effort. Build the machine, then improve the machine.

        

        

          03
          
Ship, measure, refine

          
Real data beats opinions. Get something live, watch how it behaves, and let the metrics decide what gets built next.

        

        

          04
          
Lean by design

          
Small teams, sharp tools, and software that does the heavy lifting. Stay fast by staying simple.

        

      

    


    
    

      

        

          03 — About
          
Hi, I’m Garrett.

          

            I’m a founder and builder based in Maryville, Tennessee.
            I spend most of my time at the intersection of artificial intelligence and
            everyday commerce — figuring out how far software can carry a business when
            it’s designed to operate on its own.
          

          

            My work spans applied AI and agent systems on one side and e-commerce
            operations on the other. The thread connecting them is automation: I’d
            rather build a system once than do the same task a hundred times. Lately
            that means autonomous tooling that handles real operational load — not demos,
            but software that has to actually work.
          

          

            I’m biased toward shipping. A rough system running in the real world
            teaches me more in a week than a perfect plan teaches me in a month, so I
            get things live early and let what actually happens decide the next move.
            The companies I run are, in a real sense, a testbed for that idea —
            each one a place to prove that software can carry more of the load than
            people assume.
          

          

            If you’re working on hard problems in AI, automation, or commerce —
            or just want to compare notes — I’d like to hear from you.
